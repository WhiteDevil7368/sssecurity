Common questions

Before you book

How fast can you start?+

Most patrol and officer posts start within 48–72 hours of your signed agreement. Urgent coverage can often begin the same day.

Are your officers armed?+

We provide both armed and unarmed officers. We recommend the right option after the free site assessment.

How do I see what happened on my property?+

Every patrol stop, incident and photo is logged with time and GPS, and shared in your client portal and a daily email report.

Do you require a long-term contract?+

No. We offer one-time event coverage, month-to-month patrol and discounted annual agreements.

What areas do you cover?+

We serve metro and rural areas through local franchise teams. Contact us to confirm coverage in your city.

Our story

From patrol cars to protecting communities

After years in uniform, our founders saw businesses and communities struggling with unreliable guards and no accountability. S&S Security was built to fix that — officers trained to police standards, equipped with body cams and backed by live dispatch.

2019

S&S Security founded with two patrol vehicles and six officers.

2021

Launched remote video towers and our 24/7 dispatch center.

2023

Body-worn cameras rolled out to every officer on every shift.

2026

Opened our franchise program to veterans and first responders.
